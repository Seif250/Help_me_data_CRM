Help Seif v1.17.16

Changes:
- Flexible Field 3 script steps now use {{FLEXIBLE_FIELD_3}} and resolve to the value selected in the extension popup.
- Delay steps normalized to 400 ms or 500 ms maximum, while the runner keeps smart element-presence waits.
- FL Payment writes Reason Opportunity Lost - Other only after selecting Other and before saving the loss reason.
- Sent Link now changes the stage to Registered Not Paid, opens Log a Call last, fills Subject = EG/Sales Call only, leaves the comment field untouched, and does not auto-save. Enter the comment manually and press Save.
- Follow Up task Subject selector now targets the visible task form and supports common Salesforce selector variants.
- Runtime selector resolution prefers visible matching elements to avoid selecting a hidden duplicate Subject field.
- Built-in starter workflows are refreshed once after updating the extension so the requested changes reach existing stored starter scripts.
